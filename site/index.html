Bus Tracker

Rings when your child's school bus gets near home.

For Android, with a parent account on your school board's BusPlanner portal.

Download Source on GitHub

A school day, from the morning on ↓

7:03 AM

Watching starts by itself.

A while before the pickup time, on the days you chose. Nothing to open, nothing to remember.

7:12 AM

See where the bus is.

How far, on which street, and when its GPS last reported. The map shows the bus, the stop and your zone.

7:30 AM

The bus enters your zone. The phone rings.

You draw the zone on the map: two streets before home, the corner before the stop, wherever gives you time. It rings once, one minute at most; Silence stops it.

Another morning

The bus is late.

When BusPlanner posts a delay or a cancellation, it shows in red on the run, all day.

Another morning

Your child stays home.

Turn off this morning's bell. The afternoon and tomorrow stay as usual; the bell comes back on by itself.

A snow day, a long weekend

No school until Tuesday.

Turn Automatic start off until a date. That morning, it turns back on by itself.

Any day

The schedule changed.

A new stop or a new time on BusPlanner: the app shows what changed, and you choose whether your zones and settings carry over.

2:09 PM

Same in the afternoon.

The dropoff run watches the bus home the same way. One run per child and per direction; each with its own zone.

What it needs

  • Android 10 or newer.
  • A parent account on your school board's BusPlanner portal.
  • A bus whose position BusPlanner publishes.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone. The app talks only to your BusPlanner portal, and to Android's geocoder for street names.
