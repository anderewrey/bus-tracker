The morning trip, in five steps.
1. The bus comes from the east on Highway 7 and passes This Street without turning: check 5 falls in the corner of Home zone.
2. It goes on west through the heads-up zone (check 6) and turns back at Davidsons Road (8 to 11).
3. It comes back east through the heads-up zone (check 14).
4. It turns onto This Street, into Home zone (check 16).
5. It does the loop, stops (19), and goes west to Sample School (21 to 27), where it parks: the third check at one spot (29) ends watching.
Alarm only after the heads-up, off: it rings at step 1, on the highway, and not at step 4 (already rang). On: step 1 is only crossed, and it rings at step 4.
To check before the scenarios are written
• The heads-up zone is on the way west too. Here the bus crosses it at step 2, so the heads-up sounds there, and at step 3 it says "already sounded". Is that your morning, or did the bus turn back before reaching it?
• Where does the bus turn back? Drawn at Davidsons Road, about 1.2 km west of This Street.
• The zones' shapes: Home zone takes Highway 7 from This Street to about Keizer Drive; the heads-up zone runs about 300 to 750 m west of This Street.
• The scenarios on this trip: a normal morning; heads-up then alarm; the option off and on; a network gap during the heads-up stretch; the Alarm switch off then on; the bus back in the heads-up zone after the alarm; parked at the school; a delay notice; a run without a heads-up zone.
