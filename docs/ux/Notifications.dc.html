Notifications: what each one says, when it comes and what it does
Watching · silent, ongoing
Bus TrackerB101 · Pickup 7:33 AM1.1 km · This Street[Open map] [Stop watching]
While a run is watched, one per run, updated in silence at each check. Tap or Open map → the map. Stop watching = the bell off: watching stops. Swiped away, it comes back: watching goes on.
Main zone · rings until answered
Bus TrackerB101 entered Home zone400 m · This Street[Open map] [Silence]
The bus enters the main zone, once per period. It takes the tracking notification's place and rings 1 min at most. Silence, a volume key, opening or swiping it stop the sound; watching goes on. Back to the tracking line after 1 min or when the bus leaves the zone.
BusPlanner notices · rings until answered
Bus TrackerB101: Delayed 15 minutes from original timeBus is running 15 minutes late due to traffic.B101 · posted 7:36 AM[Open map] [Silence]
BusPlanner posts a late or cancelled notice for a watched run, once per notice. A notification of its own, beside the tracking one. Watching may last longer (the delay); the run's title shows the notice in red.
Bus alerts · sounds once
Bus TrackerB101: BusPlanner reports no vehicleInformation about the vehicle associated to your child's transportation is unavailable at this time.
Watching starts and BusPlanner shows no vehicle. A warning only: watching goes on and picks the bus up once it appears. No button; a tap opens the app (the run list).
Bus alerts · sounds once
Bus TrackerBus schedule changedB101 · Pickup: see what changed
A rescan found changes (a stop, a time). Tap → Changes, to keep the configuration or take the new one.
Bus alerts · sounds once

Android refused to start watching by itself (exact alarms off, battery restrictions). Tap → the app, which starts the period under way.
